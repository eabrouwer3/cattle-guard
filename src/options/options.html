Cattle Guard

Gated sites

Every navigation to these hosts is blocked by a full-page gate until you click through — including in-app links on single-page apps. There is no grace period and nothing is remembered.

Subdomains match automatically: reddit.com also covers old.reddit.com and www.reddit.com. Full URLs are reduced to their hostname.

Site access

    Cattle Guard asks for access only to the hosts listed above. Saving a new host prompts for permission; removing one gives that access back.
